MaskLab — made for ZuCity

MaskLab is a free browser tool that fills each region of a vector shape — a yin-yang by default — or each letter of typed text, including vertical Japanese, with its own texture. Use it for flags, stickers and album covers.

Made for ZuCity, a coliving neighborhood an hour from Tokyo in the Japanese Alps. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

  • Texture fill per region, with zoom, rotate and blend
  • Built-in shapes, your own SVG, or typed text
  • Export PNG, SVG or a JSON save file
